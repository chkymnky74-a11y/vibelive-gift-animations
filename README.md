# VibeLive Gift Animations

Gift animations for the VibeLive live streaming app. No Vince anywhere in this package, his clips were left out on purpose.

## What is inside

- `gift-animation-preview.html`: open it in a browser. It shows the full gift shelf, 42 gifts in 6 families (Little Cheers, Sweet Middle, Showpieces, Cosmic, Cozy, Legends), with each gift's name, price, animation, and synthesized sound. This is the preview of the gift shelf, not the app itself.
- `gift-clips/`: 13 animated video clips of real gifts. The clips are silent. Sound is synthesized in code (Web Audio API) in the preview file, matched to each gift's action.

## Prices that are set

Rose 10, Heart 25, Star 50, Firework 100, Crown 250, Diamond 500, Rocket 750, Superstar 1499. All other gift prices are in the preview file.

## Rules for working on these gifts

- Original work only, never copied.
- No Vince, and no glam rhino gift.
- Gifts must play out fun and make sense, like a little scene. Never glorified emojis. Cheap price does not mean cheap look.
- For the top two tier sets, Legends and Cosmic, the gift says and shows the receiver's name when it plays.
- Gifts are in-app credits only, for fun, never cash and never paid out.
